# Production readiness — feature backlog (Italy car rental)

This document lists **what a full production car rental management system** typically needs in line with [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md) and Italian operational reality (**CaRGOS**, **PSD2**, **fiscal/SDI** when you go live), compared with **what this repository already includes**. Use it to **implement features one by one** (legal and operational steps are listed but must be executed with **qualified Italian counsel** and your **Questura** / **accountant**, not only in code).

**Development focus:** Prioritize **main product value** — reservations, fleet, pricing, payments, desk workflows, public quote/pay, compliance hooks (CaRGOS/SDI adapters as needed), and hardening (auth, throttles, health). **G2** (partner API — keys, throttles, **idempotent** creates) and **G3** (mock OCR + optional **HTTP** adapter + queue) exist as **partial** scaffolds in-repo; production-grade B2B identity (OAuth/mTLS) and contracted OCR vendors + DPA remain **later** unless they unblock go-live. Treat **E5** (extra PSPs), full **i18n**, and marketing-grade email as **later** when not blocking. The catalog below still lists rows for completeness; they are not equal priority to core operations.

**Related:** [PRODUCTION.md](PRODUCTION.md) (deploy, env, Docker — also **topology**, **A2/A3/H1**, **Stripe / email / S3**, **desk handover**, **GDPR**, **legal (D1/D2 agreements + e-sign)**, **pre-launch QA + rollback**, **CaRGOS worker + middleware**, **invoices / SDI**, **reconciliation / G1 reports**, **security checklist**) · [STAGING.md](STAGING.md) · [CODEBASE.md](CODEBASE.md) (current routes) · [STRUCTURE.md](STRUCTURE.md) (code map) · [MAIN-FEATURES-QUEUE.md](MAIN-FEATURES-QUEUE.md) (core build order).

### D6 — Operational checklist (CaRGOS / Italy)

**Purpose:** what **you** (operator + **qualified Italian counsel**, **Questura** / **Polizia di Stato** process) must complete so that **D4** in this repo (worker → **`cargosHttpUrl`**) actually feeds **lawful** transmissions. This is **not** legal advice; steps vary by **Questura** and product scope. **Worker env, `cargosHttpUrl`, TLS, and smoke steps:** [PRODUCTION.md § CaRGOS](PRODUCTION.md#cargos-worker-middleware-and-operations-d4-d5-d6).

1. **Eligibility & scope** — Confirm your rental model falls under CaRGOS obligations (and exclusions, e.g. certain shared-mobility models). Align **in scope** vs **out of scope** with **`Company.cargosInScope`** and desk policy.
2. **Questura / Polizia enrollment** — Complete operator onboarding for the **legal entity** (and branch rules: often **one** central submission flow). Obtain **access** to the official **CaRGOS** channel(s) / portale and any **test** environment they offer.
3. **PEC & formal communications** — Use **PEC** and documentary flows **as required** by current rules and local instructions; keep an internal **register** of submissions and outcomes (the repo stores **`CargosSubmission`** rows; your runbook defines retention and who signs off).
4. **Credentials & secrets** — Portal passwords, certificates, middleware API keys: store only in a **secret manager** (K8s secrets, cloud SM, Vault — **not** git, **not** shared `.env` in tickets). Rotate on staff change or compromise.
5. **Middleware or manual bridge** — The app POSTs **`CargosHttpAdapterPayload`** (**`specVersion` 1**, see **`@car-rental/shared`**) to **`Company.cargosHttpUrl`**. **You** provide a service (or vendor product) that maps that JSON to **official** portale / codifiche behaviour per **D.M.** and Polizia manuals **in force at go-live**.
6. **Legal interpretation** — **Timing** (“in good time” before handover), **field** requirements, and **evidence** for audits: define with counsel; wire **`cargosCutoffMinutesBeforePickup`** and **handover override** (desk **ADMIN** / **BRANCH_MANAGER**, audited — see [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md) §3.6) accordingly.
7. **Staging vs production** — [STAGING.md](STAGING.md): no **production** CaRGOS credentials on staging; use **`cargosEnvironment`** **TEST** and Polizia **test** paths only when available.
8. **Monitoring** — Alert on **`FAILED`** **`CargosSubmission`** and worker exits ([TECH_STACK.md](TECH_STACK.md) §8); **A6** telemetry remains largely **ops**.

**Relation to the repo:** after D6, you still run the same **API + worker + desk**; you change **who** receives the HTTP POST and **how** that service talks to Polizia — not the core domain model.
